## Repository-generated prototypes

`game/Content/Characters/Prototype/NovaFemale.obj` and `RexMale.obj` are generated in this repository by `tools/generate_3d_characters.py`; they are not copied from the third-party sources above. They are prototype meshes and do not carry third-party attribution requirements.

## Shipping rule

Do not ship an external asset until its exact downloaded package, license text, author, source URL, and modification status are recorded here and in the release manifest. Keep license files with packaged distributions where required.
