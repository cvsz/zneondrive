# Commercial Scope Options — Client Discussion

This document is a scoping framework, not a binding quotation.

## Option A — Playable Vertical Slice

**Objective:** create a high-confidence product milestone that can be shown to executives, investors, publishers, or internal stakeholders.

### Deliverables
- Unreal playable client
- Garage 17 + Foundry 9 graybox-to-polish scope agreed with client
- MQ001–MQ012
- starter vehicle rebuild loop
- First Ignition hero moment
- legal time trial
- underground race proof
- save/reconnect
- basic service plane and PostgreSQL persistence
- telemetry dashboard for the slice
- acceptance test pack

### Planning window
8–12 weeks after fidelity/platform scope lock.

### Primary variables
- vehicle physics fidelity,
- environment asset quality,
- cinematic/voice scope,
- number of target platforms,
- simultaneous player target.

## Option B — Online Alpha

Includes Option A plus:
- identity/account service,
- durable progression,
- multiplayer district,
- social presence,
- crew MVP,
- event registration,
- relationship/faction persistence,
- anti-cheat telemetry,
- admin/live-ops basics,
- load/soak,
- backup/restore drill.

### Planning window
4–6 months from approved alpha scope.

## Option C — Full Production Program

Includes:
- seven campaign chapters,
- 100 main quests implemented,
- launch districts,
- race disciplines,
- seasonal NOVA GRAND PRIX,
- crew championship,
- moderation/customer support,
- analytics/live ops,
- privacy and security hardening,
- platform certification,
- HA/DR and production evidence.

### Planning model
Multi-phase program. Final schedule and commercial quote require platform, fidelity, content-volume, localization, concurrency, hosting, and IP/asset ownership decisions.

## Suggested team for Vertical Slice

- Product/Game Director — 1
- Technical Director / Lead Engineer — 1
- Unreal Gameplay Engineers — 2–3
- Backend/Online Engineer — 1
- Technical Artist / Environment — 1–2
- UI/UX — 0.5–1
- QA — 1
- Narrative/Quest Designer — 0.5–1

Roles may be combined in a compact team.

## Commercial questions to resolve

1. Fixed scope, time-and-materials, or co-development?
2. Who owns source code, art assets, music, and IP?
3. Is source transfer required at each milestone?
4. Is hosting included or separate?
5. Are console certification costs included?
6. Are localization, voice acting, and motion capture included?
7. Is post-launch live operation part of the contract?
