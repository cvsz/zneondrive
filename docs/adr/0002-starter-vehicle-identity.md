# ADR-0002: Preserve the Starter Vehicle as a Long-Lived Identity Object

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-12

## Context

The story and product identity rely on the player's first broken vehicle becoming personally and narratively significant rather than disposable loot.

## Decision

The starter vehicle can be rebuilt and reconfigured across multiple disciplines and remains viable through long-term progression. It has stable identity, provenance, history, and build revisions.

Routine damage or economy systems must not permanently destroy the unique story vehicle.

## Consequences

Progression emphasizes parts, build revisions, mastery, reputation, and specialization instead of mandatory vehicle replacement. VIP garage capacity expands collection without making the starter vehicle obsolete.
