# Pets & Companions

## Core rule

Companions provide exploration, narrative and social value. They do not add ranked competitive vehicle buffs.

## Launch archetypes

The canonical catalog includes Luna and additional companion archetypes under `design/catalog/pets.json`.

## Allowed utility

Examples:
- discovery cues,
- exploration interaction,
- narrative beats,
- cosmetic/social presence,
- non-ranked convenience where fairness is preserved.

## Prohibited ranked advantage

Companions must not provide hidden:
- horsepower,
- grip,
- acceleration,
- durability,
- timing advantage,
- ranked reward multiplier.

## Persistence

Future implementation should persist:
- owned/unlocked companions,
- selected companion,
- narrative/progression state where needed.

## Safety/content

Pet interaction should avoid mechanics that incentivize neglect/harm for optimal competitive play.

## Evidence

Companion runtime remains a future content-alpha item until Luna's discovery/utility loop is playable and persisted.
