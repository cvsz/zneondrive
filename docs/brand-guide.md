# Brand Guide — PROJECT: NEON DRIVE

## Core identity

**Name:** PROJECT: NEON DRIVE  
**World:** NOVA CITY · 2097  
**Motto:** **You don't own the road. You earn it.**

## Visual direction

The brand combines:
- midnight futuristic cityscapes,
- wet asphalt and reflective surfaces,
- cyan/magenta neon,
- mechanical/garage texture,
- premium racing key-art composition,
- human-scale street culture beneath corporate megastructures.

## Suggested palette

- Midnight: `#070A12`
- Deep Navy: `#0B1020`
- Neon Cyan: `#62F4FF`
- Neon Magenta: `#FF4FD8`
- Electric Lime: `#A7FF4F`
- Signal Amber: `#FFC857`
- Danger: `#FF6B7A`

## Typography behavior

Use a wide/technical sans-serif for headings and a readable neutral sans-serif for body copy. Avoid decorative fonts for long text.

## Messaging hierarchy

1. PROJECT: NEON DRIVE
2. You don't own the road. You earn it.
3. Open World RPG · Vehicle Building · Racing · Social
4. NOVA CITY · 2097

## Repository assets

The repository banner lives under `assets/`. Social preview exports should use a 2:1 composition, preferably 1280×640.

## Usage rules

- Do not use real automotive manufacturer trademarks/logos in original key art without permission.
- Avoid implying final production fidelity from concept art.
- Mark concept/presentation art as such where context could be ambiguous.
- Maintain readable title-safe space for GitHub/social crops.
