# Glossary

**Authoritative state** — state accepted by the trusted server/service rather than the player client.

**Build revision** — immutable numbered snapshot of a vehicle configuration accepted by the service.

**DRIVE ZERO** — central story/technology program capable of controlling network-connected mobility systems.

**Evidence-gated** — status remains incomplete until the exact required build/test/operational evidence exists.

**First Ignition** — early-game milestone where the rebuilt starter prototype first becomes operational.

**Foundry 9** — industrial launch district containing Garage 17 and early rebuild gameplay.

**Garage 17** — opening garage and key early narrative location associated with Maya Voss.

**Gameplay plane** — Unreal dedicated-server layer responsible for moment-to-moment authoritative gameplay.

**Gameplay ticket** — short-lived, one-time credential issued to a player session and redeemed by an authorized gameplay server.

**NOVA CITY** — persistent primary world setting in 2097.

**Operation ID** — stable idempotency identifier bound to a durable mutation/retry.

**PROJECT: NEON DRIVE** — project/game title.

**Roadworthy** — durable vehicle/story state indicating the starter vehicle passed the first rebuild milestone.

**Service plane** — Go/PostgreSQL authority for durable account, ownership, inventory, quest and progression state.

**Stable ID** — published machine-readable identifier that should remain compatible across content/runtime versions.

**Starter lineage** — property identifying the player's original story vehicle and protecting its provenance.

**Vertical slice** — deliberately limited but end-to-end playable section used to prove product feel and production assumptions.

**VIP** — monetization/convenience tier constrained by the non-pay-to-win fairness contract.
