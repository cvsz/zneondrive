# Gameplay Systems v0.1

## Progression state

- **Money** — general in-world purchasing power.
- **XP** — character progression.
- **Reputation** — public/scene credibility.
- **Faction standing** — political trust.
- **Relationship state** — NPC-specific trust.
- **Blueprint knowledge** — build capability.
- **Vehicle reputation** — fame attached to a specific vehicle identity.

No real-money currency is defined in v0.1.

## Vehicle ownership and history

Each vehicle must support a stable Vehicle ID, current owner, builders, chassis/prototype lineage, build revision history, repair history, race/championship history, reputation, and story provenance.

A race result references the exact build revision used.

## Build roles

Street / Drift / Circuit / Drag / Off-road / Delivery / Hybrid.

Reconfiguration must create trade-offs; no build should dominate every discipline.

## Damage and maintenance

Support visible condition, component wear, repair choices, and workshop depth without turning maintenance into punitive chores. Unique story vehicles cannot be permanently destroyed through routine play.

## Racing integrity

Authoritative checks must validate event eligibility, permitted parts, build revision, start state, route/checkpoints, impossible movement, finish order/time, penalties, and disconnect/rejoin policy.

## Reputation

Reputation is not a morality meter. A player may be respected by Iron Wolves and distrusted by NOVA Authority, or the reverse.

## Relationships

Relationship changes are event-driven and threshold-based. Major transitions require authored story moments.

## Pets

Pets support exploration and discovery, not race stats. Critical progression always provides an alternative route.

## Crew system

Crew identity, roles, crew reputation, crew garage, team challenges, ranking, qualifiers, and crew championships form the social competitive loop.

## VIP fairness contract

VIP may increase garage capacity, blueprint/storage capacity, saved loadout slots, and cosmetic/social options.

VIP must not increase engine power, grip, braking, durability, ranked reward multipliers, matchmaking priority, or hidden handling assistance unavailable through normal accessibility settings.

## Seasons

A season may add qualifiers, championships, faction storylets, world events, blueprints/parts, social themes, crew goals, and new story beats without erasing main-story progress or invalidating permanent player vehicles.
