# Moderation & Player Safety

**Status:** policy baseline for future social/live-service implementation.

## Surfaces requiring moderation

Planned social surfaces may include:
- player names,
- crew names/descriptions,
- chat,
- car-meet/social venue communication,
- user-generated text,
- reports,
- profile/garage presentation.

## Minimum controls before public social alpha

- block/mute,
- report flow with category and context,
- moderation case/audit trail,
- sanctions with reason and duration,
- appeal/support path,
- rate limits/spam controls,
- privileged moderator RBAC.

## Safety categories

The system should be capable of handling:
- harassment/threats,
- hate/abuse,
- sexual harassment,
- doxxing/personal information,
- scams/phishing,
- cheating/exploit promotion,
- spam,
- inappropriate names/content.

## Age-rating context

The game's 18+ positioning allows mature fictional themes, but it does not remove requirements for harassment prevention, privacy protection or illegal-content response.

## Evidence

Moderation readiness requires implemented tools, operator procedures, test cases and escalation ownership. A policy document alone is not a moderation system.
